DATASET DOWNLOAD LINKS

AutoLand collapsed-buildings dataset
https://autoland.cs.uni-freiburg.de/
Approximately 1.28 million synthetic images with RGB, depth, surface normals, semantics, and camera poses.

RELLIS-3D dataset and code
https://github.com/unmannedlab/RELLIS-3D
Includes labeled RGB images and an explicit rubble class. The collection also has other sensor modalities, but the RGB data can be used independently.

RUGD dataset
https://rugd.vision/
Labeled RGB video frames for unstructured ground navigation.

The datasets themselves were not placed in this ZIP because they are large. Their papers and official download links are included.
