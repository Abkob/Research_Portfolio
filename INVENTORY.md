# Curation, attribution, and source boundary

This public portfolio was assembled from local project repositories, reports, presentations, research plans, evidence maps, source indexes, and selected engineering files. It is not a bulk upload of the laptop.

## Included

- Original project summaries with explicit stage labels
- Public-safe notebooks and historical code where provenance is sufficiently clear
- Original or team-attributed reports and presentations
- Smart-liner TeX sources, CAD/PCB artifacts, and project-generated renders
- Research plans, source indexes, data-access notes, and archive checksums
- An anonymized NeuroSense dashboard reconstruction
- A catalog of extracurricular reference books, kept separate from authored research

## Attribution rules

- Team artifacts are labelled as team work; a file's presence does not imply sole authorship.
- The smart-liner report explicitly labels the Q6 section as **Individual Assignment — Abdulrahman Kobeissi** and records the team's role boundaries.
- NeuroSense remains attributed to Team Beirut 332 because the archive does not assign every component to one individual.
- AI-assisted research synthesis is disclosed as such and is not presented as peer-reviewed publication.

## Not uploaded

- Raw ECG, EEG, ECoG, imaging, speech, or other human-subject recordings
- Participant/student metadata, workbooks, identifiers, databases, exports, logs, or embeddings
- Model weights, checkpoints, serialized scalers, or package/build caches
- Downloaded journal PDFs, commercial books/EPUBs, or extracted publisher figures
- API keys, environment files, connection strings, or deployment secrets
- Private meeting transcripts and operational archives
- Unclear-license derivative notebooks or tutorial code presented as original work

## Duplicate and merge decisions

- The small `MotorBrace(Ab)` folder was a subset of the fuller MotorBrace archive and was merged conceptually into one project.
- `Project_62625` and its large unnamed model folder were one Attention U-Net experiment; the weight file was excluded. The later WeedBot adaptation is represented inside AARS.
- Duplicate/near-duplicate BCI Week 1 notebooks were not added again; the existing curated versions remain canonical.
- Duplicate reference-book copies and edition variants are consolidated in `REFERENCE_LIBRARY.md` instead of inflating the catalog.

## Why

A professional research portfolio should be inspectable without violating privacy, data-use agreements, collaborator attribution, copyright, or security. Project files show the work; stage labels and exclusions keep the claims accurate.
